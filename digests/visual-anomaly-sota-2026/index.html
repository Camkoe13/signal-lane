New State-of-the-Art in Visual Anomaly Detection: Zero-Shot Methods Close Gap with Supervised Approaches

September 14, 2026

What Matters

Foundation-model zero-shot detection now matches supervised methods without requiring domain-specific training data.

Key Takeaways

  • Zero-shot methods can match supervised performance on industrial benchmarks.
  • New architectures reduce false positives in manufacturing settings.
  • Smaller models enable deployment on industrial cameras.

Foundation models are reshaping industrial visual inspection. Zero-shot systems generalize from natural-language descriptions of defects, reducing the need to retrain whenever a product line changes.
